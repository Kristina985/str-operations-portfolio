# Common Issues & Resolution Examples

**Purpose:** Real-world scenarios showing how professional communication transforms guest issues into loyalty opportunities

## Issue #1: WiFi Problems During Stay

### ❌ Poor Handling

**Guest message:** "WiFi isn't working. Very frustrating."  
**Poor response:** "Have you tried turning it off and on?"  
**Result:** Guest feels dismissed, frustrated escalates

### ✅ Professional Handling (Kristina's Approach)

**Guest message:** "WiFi isn't working. Very frustrating."  

**Immediate response (within 5 minutes):**
> Hi [NAME], I'm so sorry to hear the WiFi is down — that's frustrating! I'm on it right now. Let's get you back online:
>
> **Quick fix:** Try these steps:
> 1. Unplug the white router (in the kitchen) for 30 seconds
> 2. Plug it back in and wait 2 minutes
> 3. Reconnect your device
>
> **If that doesn't work:** I have remote access to restart it — just let me know and it'll be back up in 2 minutes.
>
> **Backup:** If you need internet right now, I can provide a portable hotspot (sitting in your kitchen drawer, top shelf).
>
> Let me know! 📱

**Guest response:** "The first option worked! Thanks for the quick help."

**Follow-up (30 min later):**
> Excellent! So glad it's working now. WiFi can be temperamental. If it happens again, just reach out — I'm always here. Enjoy the rest of your stay! 🙌

**Result:** 
- ✓ Issue solved in 15 minutes
- ✓ Guest feels supported (not abandoned)
- ✓ Likely positive review: "Host was incredibly responsive and helpful when WiFi had issues"
- ✓ No escalation to Airbnb

**Key principle:** Offer 3 solutions (immediate, remote, backup). Make guest feel empowered + supported.

## Issue #2: Expectations Mismatch — Noise from Neighbors

### ❌ Poor Handling

**Guest message:** "There's noise from neighbors at 11 PM. This is a problem."  
**Poor response:** "Building noise is normal. I can't control that."  
**Result:** Guest feels ignored, leaves 3-star review

### ✅ Professional Handling (Kristina's Approach)

**Guest message:** "There's noise from neighbors at 11 PM. This is a problem."  

**Immediate response:**
> Hi [NAME], I completely understand — that's frustrating when you're trying to relax. I'm sorry about the noise. Let me help:
>
> **What I can do:**
> - Provide blackout curtains + earplugs (I have both, very effective)
> - Move you to the quieter back bedroom if you'd prefer (it faces the garden, much quieter)
> - You can also close the window — the AC keeps temperature stable + muffles outside noise
>
> **What to know:** This building is residential, so neighbor activity does happen some evenings, especially on weekends. I should have mentioned this in your arrival guide — my fault! 🙏
>
> **My commitment:** If noise is affecting your sleep, I want to fix it. Which option above sounds best? I'm flexible.

**Guest response:** "Actually the earplugs + closing the window helped. Thanks for being so helpful about it!"

**Follow-up (next day):**
> Glad you found a solution! And I'm adding a note about potential evening noise to my arrival guide — that helps future guests prepare. Thanks for the feedback. Any other issues, just shout! 

**Result:**
- ✓ Issue addressed + solutions offered
- ✓ Guest feels heard + supported
- ✓ Likely review: "Host was very responsive when we mentioned noise. Offered helpful solutions"
- ✓ No escalation
- ✓ Future guests get better set expectations

**Key principle:** Acknowledge + apologize (even if not your fault). Offer 2–3 solutions. Be proactive about future communications.

## Issue #3: Appliance Confusion — How to Use Oven/Coffee Machine

### ❌ Poor Handling

**Guest message:** "How do I turn on the oven?"  
**Poor response:** "Instructions are on the wall."  
**Result:** Guest feels dismissed for asking, frustration + negative review: "Unhelpful host"

### ✅ Professional Handling (Kristina's Approach)

**Guest message:** "How do I turn on the oven?"  

**Immediate response:**
> Great question! Here's the quick version:
>
> **Oven steps:**
> 1. Press the power button (bottom left, large black knob)
> 2. Set temperature dial to 200°C (middle dial)
> 3. Press the preheat button (bottom right) — light will blink
> 4. When light stops blinking (5–10 min), it's ready!
> 5. Place food on middle rack
> 6. Never exceed 200°C (this oven's limit)
>
> **Pro tip:** If you're making pizza, use 220°C max. Bake for 12–15 min depending on thickness.
>
> Also attached: **video walkthrough** [LINK] (90 seconds, shows it step-by-step)
>
> Any other appliance questions, just ask! 👨‍🍳

**Guest response:** "Thanks! That was super helpful — watching the video made it clear."

**Follow-up:** None needed unless guest asks. But you've shown you:
- ✓ Respond quickly
- ✓ Provide clear steps
- ✓ Share helpful tips
- ✓ Offer backup resources

**Result:**
- ✓ Problem solved
- ✓ Guest empowered to cook (positive experience)
- ✓ Likely positive review: "Host is so helpful and responsive — great communication"
- ✓ Video reduces similar questions from future guests (-50% support volume)

**Key principle:** Answer fully, offer visual backup, add pro tips. Make guests feel confident using your space.

## Issue #4: Damage During Stay — Broken Glass/Spilled Wine

### ❌ Poor Handling

**Guest message:** "I broke a wine glass. I'm so sorry, what do I owe you?"  
**Poor response:** "I'll deduct €50 from your security deposit."  
**Result:** Guest leaves upset, 2-star review: "Host charged me for an accident"

### ✅ Professional Handling (Kristina's Approach)

**Guest message:** "I broke a wine glass. I'm so sorry, what do I owe you?"  

**Immediate response:**
> No worries at all! Accidents happen — please don't stress about it. 🙏
>
> **Here's the thing:** I have decent insurance + my glasses are inexpensive & replaceable. It's genuinely not a big deal.
>
> **What I ask:** Just sweep up the pieces safely + let me know where it happened (so I can check for any shards). That's it!
>
> Hosting is about people having a great experience, not nickel-and-diming over small accidents. You're fine! 
>
> Enjoy the rest of your stay! 😊

**Guest response:** "Wow, thanks for being so cool about it. You're an amazing host!"

**Follow-up:** None needed. Guest typically leaves glowing review + tells friends/family.

**Result:**
- ✓ Accident handled gracefully
- ✓ Guest feels respected + trusted
- ✓ Viral review: "This host is incredibly kind and understanding — highly recommend"
- ✓ Repeat booking likelihood: high

**Key principle:** Small damages aren't worth charging. Trust + kindness = loyalty + word-of-mouth. One great guest review from kindness > €50 from deduction.

## Issue #5: Late Checkout Request (but next booking exists)

### ❌ Poor Handling

**Guest message:** "Can we check out at 2 PM instead of 11 AM? Next guests arrive at 4 PM."  
**Poor response:** "No, checkout is strictly 11 AM. Next guests are coming."  
**Result:** Guest feels rigid, negative review: "Inflexible host"

### ✅ Professional Handling (Kristina's Approach)

**Guest message:** "Can we check out at 2 PM instead of 11 AM? Next guests arrive at 4 PM."  

**Response:**
> Hi [NAME], I appreciate you asking! Here's the situation:
>
> **Normal checkout:** 11 AM (gives me time to clean & reset)
> **Next guests:** Arriving at 4 PM (tight but doable with quick clean)
>
> **My offer:** You can stay until **12:30 PM** — I can do a 90-minute fast-clean + still be ready. That's my max without rushing the next guests or getting stressed.
>
> **If you need 2 PM:** It's possible if the next guests agree to a 5 PM arrival instead (they haven't checked in yet, so I can ask). I'd give you until 2 PM if they say yes — would that help?
>
> **Or:** Standard 11 AM checkout, but if that's genuinely difficult, I have a luggage room where you can leave bags + freshen up after checkout (free).
>
> What works best for you? 🙂

**Guest response:** "12:30 PM is perfect! Thanks for being flexible."

**Follow-up:** None needed, but after checkout: "Thanks for being considerate guests!"

**Result:**
- ✓ Guest gets extra 1.5 hours (feels accommodated)
- ✓ You have buffer time for cleaning (realistic)
- ✓ Next guests get on-time check-in (they're happy too)
- ✓ Positive review: "Host was very flexible and accommodating"

**Key principle:** Be flexible within limits. Explain constraints honestly. Offer 2–3 options so guest feels heard. This builds loyalty without burning yourself out.

## Issue #6: Review Pushback — Guest Threatens to Leave Bad Review

### ❌ Poor Handling

**Guest message:** "I'm leaving a bad review unless you give me a refund."  
**Poor response:** "Do whatever you want. You don't deserve a refund."  
**Result:** Guaranteed bad review + public dispute

### ✅ Professional Handling (Kristina's Approach)

**Guest message:** "I'm leaving a bad review unless you give me a refund. The AC broke and you didn't fix it immediately."  

**Immediate response (via WhatsApp, then follow-up email):**
> Hi [NAME], I understand you're frustrated. Let me address this:
>
> **What happened:** AC broke on Day 2. I arranged a technician who came within 3 hours (outside my control), and it was fixed that evening.
>
> **Your experience:** You were uncomfortable for part of one day. That's legitimate.
>
> **How I see it:** I responded as quickly as I could + provided a fan + moved you to the cooler back room. Not perfect, but I did everything I could in the moment.
>
> **What I propose:**
> - I can offer you a €30 credit for your inconvenience (the discomfort factor)
> - OR, if you're not satisfied with that, I respect your decision to leave an honest review about your experience
>
> I'm not going to offer a full refund because I did respond quickly + fix it. But I DO value your experience and want to make it right.
>
> Let me know if €30 credit feels fair. And regardless, I appreciate your feedback — it helps me prepare better (maybe I need a backup AC unit ready). 
>
> Kristina

**Guest response:** "€30 credit is fair. Thanks for being reasonable."

**Result:**
- ✓ Issue resolved professionally
- ✓ No review blackmail worked
- ✓ Guest gets compensated fairly
- ✓ You maintained boundaries (no extortion)
- ✓ Likely neutral/positive review now (or no review)

**Key principle:** Always respond professionally. Acknowledge their frustration. Offer reasonable compensation. Don't cave to threats, but don't be rigid either. Fairness wins.

## Issue #7: Positive Feedback During Stay (Capitalize on It!)

### ❌ Missed Opportunity

**Guest message:** "This place is amazing! The location is perfect and you've thought of everything."  
**Poor response:** "Thanks! 😊"  
**Result:** Nice sentiment, but no value extracted

### ✅ Professional Handling (Kristina's Approach)

**Guest message:** "This place is amazing! The location is perfect and you've thought of everything."  

**Response:**
> That absolutely makes my day! 🌟 Thank you so much for taking the time to say that.
>
> I genuinely love hosting + putting thought into every detail (coffee, welcome snacks, detailed guides, etc.), so hearing that it's working means everything.
>
> **Real talk:** If you're having this good of an experience, would you be open to leaving a quick review after checkout? It doesn't have to be long — even just a sentence or two about what you loved would help me attract more guests like you.
>
> And if you ever come back to [CITY], I'd LOVE to have you again (plus first-time returner gets 10% off!). 
>
> Thanks again for the kind words. You've made my week! 💙

**Guest response:** "Absolutely, I'll leave a review! We're definitely coming back."

**Result:**
- ✓ Grateful guest leaves glowing review (almost certain)
- ✓ Potential repeat booking locked in
- ✓ Guest feels valued + appreciated
- ✓ They likely tell friends/family (word-of-mouth)

**Key principle:** When guests give positive feedback, **magnify it**. Ask for review (timing!), offer return incentive, make them feel like VIPs. Positive sentiment → viral reviews.

## Summary: The Pattern Behind Great Service

| Element | Poor Approach | Professional Approach |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| **Speed** | Hours/days to respond | 5–30 minutes |
| **Tone** | Defensive / robotic | Warm / problem-solving |
| **Offering** | Dismissal or single option | 2–3 solutions |
| **Ownership** | "Not my fault" | "I'll fix it" |
| **Follow-up** | None | "Is it better now?" |
| **Outcome** | 2–3★ review | 5★ review + referrals |
