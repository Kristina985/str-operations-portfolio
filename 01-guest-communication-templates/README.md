# Guest Communication Templates

Professional, multilingual guest communication templates used across all stages of the guest lifecycle. These templates have been tested and refined over 10+ years of hosting.

## Overview

Clear, structured communication is the foundation of excellent guest reviews. This folder contains ready-to-use templates for:
- Pre-arrival guidance and expectation-setting
- Mid-stay check-ins and issue resolution
- Check-out coordination
- Review requests and follow-up

**Key principle:** Guests should never need to ask for information that can be provided proactively.

## Templates Included

### Pre-Arrival Communication
- **Welcome message** — Sent immediately after booking confirmation
- **Arrival guide** — 48 hours before check-in with detailed navigation, access instructions, and property information
- **Weather & local tips** — Contextual updates based on season and guest profile

### During-Stay Communication
- **Mid-stay check-in** — Day 2 or 3, confirms guest comfort and offers support
- **Issue resolution templates** — WiFi problems, appliance queries, noise concerns, local recommendations

### Check-Out Communication
- **Departure reminder** — 24 hours before checkout with process and timing
- **Flexible check-out offer** — Conditional on next booking schedule

### Post-Stay Communication
- **Review request** — Sent 24 hours after checkout, timing optimised for positive engagement
- **Thank you + referral** — Follow-up for high satisfaction, encourages recommendations

## Language Support

All templates available in:
- **English** (EN) — primary market
- **Croatian** (HR) — local context, cultural sensitivity
- **Spanish** (ES) — growing Latin American traveller segment

## Performance Metrics

Templates in this folder have generated:
- **~99% 5-star review rate** across 100+ reviews
- **100% response-to-communication rate** — zero unresolved guest issues
- **Guest recommendation rate:** 60%+ of reviews contain explicit recommendations to friends/family

## How to Use These

1. **Copy the template** to your messaging platform (Airbnb, email, WhatsApp)
2. **Personalise with property details** — name, address, specific amenities
3. **Adjust timing** — send pre-arrival 48-72 hours ahead, mid-stay on day 2-3, checkout reminder 24 hours prior
4. **Track response rates** — update templates based on guest feedback

## Best Practices Embedded in These Templates

- **Proactive information** — everything guests need is provided without them asking
- **Mobile-friendly formatting** — readable on small screens in transit
- **Tone consistency** — warm, professional, multilingual clarity
- **Timing optimisation** — sent at moments when guests most need the information
- **Visual aids** — links to videos, maps, or photo guides for complex instructions
