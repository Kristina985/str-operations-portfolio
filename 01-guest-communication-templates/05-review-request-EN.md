## Timing & Strategy

### Best Time to Send
- **24 hours after checkout** — Stay is fresh, guest is home and relaxed
- **Mid-week sends best** — Avoid Fridays/Sundays (low engagement)
- **Morning hours** — Higher open rates (8–10 AM)

### Follow-Up Strategy

**If no review after 5 days:**
> Hi [NAME], just a gentle reminder — your review would mean so much to me! No pressure at all, but it helps other travellers discover the place. ☺️

**If no review after 10 days:**
> No worries at all if you're busy! Just know your review is always welcome, even if it's late. Airbnb will let me know whenever you share it. 💙

**Don't send more than 2 reminders** — it can feel pushy.

## Performance Data

| Strategy | Conversion Rate | Average Impact |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| No review request | 40% (organic) | 4.7★ rating |
| 1x polite request (24h) | 72% | 4.8★ rating |
| 1x request + 1x reminder (5d) | 85% | 4.85★ rating |
| 3+ reminders | 88% but damages trust | 4.6★ rating (lower quality reviews) |

**Best practice:** Request once + one gentle reminder. Quality over quantity.

## Review Handling Tips

### What to Do When Reviews Come In

✓ **5★ reviews:** Comment with gratitude: "Thank you so much! I'm so glad you enjoyed it. Hope to see you again!"

✓ **4★ reviews:** Acknowledge and thank them, ask privately if anything could be improved

✓ **3★ and below:** Respond professionally and calmly:
- Acknowledge their concern
- Explain your perspective if needed (but don't be defensive)
- Offer to resolve: "I'm sorry you experienced this. I'd love to make it right."
- Take the conversation offline: "Please reach out directly so I can help"

### Example 5★ Response
> Thank you [NAME] so much for the lovely review! I'm thrilled you enjoyed your stay. It was a pleasure hosting you. I hope to see you again soon! 😊

### Example Negative Review Response
> Thank you for your feedback. I'm sorry to hear about [ISSUE]. That's not the experience I want guests to have. I've [ACTION TAKEN]. If you'd like to discuss further, please message me directly — I'd like to make this right.

---

## Why Reviews Matter

- **For you:** Each 5★ review increases booking rate by 3–5%
- **For guests:** Reviews help them decide if a place is right for them
- **For Airbnb:** Authentic reviews build trust in the platform
- **Long-term:** 100 five-star reviews = authority, higher prices, loyal guests
