# 🧩 Common Issues & Resolution Guide
Standardized responses and actions for the most frequent STR operational issues.

---

## 1. Wi‑Fi Not Working
### Symptoms
- Guest reports no connection  
- Slow speed  
- Devices not connecting  

### Resolution Steps
1. Ask guest to restart router (photo included).  
2. Confirm lights on router (Power / WAN / Wi‑Fi).  
3. Run remote provider diagnostics (if available).  
4. If unresolved → escalate to provider.  
5. Update guest every 10–15 minutes.

### Communication Template
“Thank you for letting me know — I’m checking this right away.  
Let’s try a quick reset together, it usually solves the issue.”

---

## 2. Cleaning Issues
### Symptoms
- Missed spots  
- Dust  
- Trash left  
- Linen not replaced  

### Resolution Steps
1. Apologize and acknowledge.  
2. Contact cleaner immediately.  
3. Offer guest a time window for correction.  
4. Follow up after cleaner finishes.  
5. Document issue for cleaner performance tracking.

### Communication Template
“I’m really sorry this wasn’t perfect — I’ve already contacted the cleaner and we’ll fix it right away.”

---

## 3. Noise Complaints
### Symptoms
- Street noise  
- Neighbors  
- Construction  

### Resolution Steps
1. Apologize and validate guest’s experience.  
2. Provide mitigation (earplugs, white noise app).  
3. Contact building manager if needed.  
4. Offer room switch (if available).  
5. Follow up next morning.

### Communication Template
“I completely understand how frustrating noise can be — let me help you make the stay more comfortable.”

---

## 4. Keybox / Entry Problems
### Symptoms
- Guest can’t find keybox  
- Code not working  
- Door stuck  

### Resolution Steps
1. Send photo of keybox location.  
2. Confirm correct code.  
3. Guide guest step‑by‑step.  
4. If stuck → send backup code or meet in person (if possible).  
5. Document issue for maintenance.

### Communication Template
“No worries — I’ll guide you step by step. Here’s a photo of the exact location.”

---

## 5. Damage Reports
### Symptoms
- Broken glass  
- Stain  
- Minor accidental damage  

### Resolution Steps
1. Use friendly, non‑stress tone.  
2. Ask for a photo.  
3. Assess if repair is needed.  
4. Apply damage policy (no stress for small items).  
5. Thank guest for honesty.

### Communication Template
“Thank you for letting me know — these things happen, no stress at all.”

---

## 6. Overbooking or Calendar Sync Issues
### Symptoms
- Double booking  
- Cleaner not notified  
- Wrong check‑in time  

### Resolution Steps
1. Identify source (Airbnb / Booking / manual).  
2. Contact guest immediately.  
3. Offer alternative or compensation.  
4. Update calendar + cleaner.  
5. Document incident.

### Communication Template
“I’m really sorry for the mix‑up — here’s what I can offer to make this right.”

---

## Summary
This guide demonstrates:
- calm problem‑solving  
- structured decision‑making  
- guest‑first communication  
- ability to handle pressure
