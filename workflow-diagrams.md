## 2. Issue Resolution Workflow

### ASCII Diagram

[Guest Reports Issue]
          |
          v

          |
          v
[Choose Action Path]
   |
   |-- Wi‑Fi → Troubleshoot → Provider Contact → Update Guest
   |-- Cleaning → Contact Cleaner → Confirm Return → Update Guest
   |-- Noise → Apology → Mitigation → Follow‑Up
   |-- Damage → Friendly Policy → Resolution
          |
          v
[Confirm Resolution]
          |
          v
[Close Case + Document]
