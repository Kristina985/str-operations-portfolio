# 🔄 Before & After Scenarios
Real examples showing how operational issues were transformed into positive guest experiences.

---

## 1. Guest Confusion About Check-In
### ❌ Before
Guest arrives and messages:  
“We’re here but don’t know where the entrance is.”

### ✅ After
Clear pre-arrival message sent 24h before arrival with:
• Map screenshot  
• Entrance photo  
• Keybox location  
• Step-by-step instructions  

🎯 Result: Zero confusion, smooth arrival, 5★ review.

---

## 2. High Volume of Repetitive Questions
### ❌ Before
Guests repeatedly ask:
- “How do we get to Old Town?”
- “Where do we park?”
- “What’s the Wi-Fi password?”

### ✅ After
Created:
• Welcome booklet  
• Automated WhatsApp welcome message  
• Visual map with directions  
• Wi-Fi card placed on table  

🎯 Result: Guest questions reduced by 50%.

---

## 3. Cleaning Delays
### ❌ Before
Cleaner forgets schedule → apartment not ready on time.

### ✅ After
Introduced:
• Google Calendar reminders  
• WhatsApp automated cleaner notifications  
• Standardized cleaning checklist  

🎯 Result: 0 missed cleanings in 90 days.

---

## 4. Guest Complains About Noise
### ❌ Before
Guest messages at midnight:  
“It’s too loud, we can’t sleep.”

### ✅ After
Immediate response:
• Apology  
• Earplugs delivered  
• Offered quiet-side room (if available)  
• Follow-up message in the morning  

🎯 Result: Guest stayed, left positive review mentioning “amazing host”.

---

## 5. Pricing Not Optimized
### ❌ Before
Static pricing → low occupancy in shoulder season.

### ✅ After
Introduced:
• Dynamic pricing logic  
• Competitor monitoring  
• Weekly adjustments  

🎯 Result: +18% occupancy, +12% revenue.

---

## 6. Guest Reports Minor Damage
### ❌ Before
Stressful communication, unclear expectations.

### ✅ After
Created:
• Friendly damage policy  
• “No stress” tone  
• Clear instructions for reporting  

🎯 Result: Guests report issues honestly, no conflicts.
