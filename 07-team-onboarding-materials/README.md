# Team Onboarding Materials

**Purpose:** Replicable training materials for onboarding new team members or delegating tasks

---

## New Team Member Onboarding Checklist

### Day 1: Welcome & Foundation

- [ ] **Welcome call** (30 min) — Overview of role, expectations, communication protocols
- [ ] **Read:** Main README.md + QUICK-REFERENCE.md
- [ ] **Access:** Grant access to email, WhatsApp, Airbnb, CRM tools
- [ ] **Review:** Guest Communication Templates folder (all templates)
- [ ] **Watch:** Property walkthrough video (if applicable)
- [ ] **Q&A:** Open questions session

### Day 2–3: Core SOPs

- [ ] **Study:** Check-In/Check-Out SOP (02-checkin-checkout-sop)
- [ ] **Understand:** Response time commitments & SLAs
- [ ] **Review:** Common Issues & Resolution Examples (what to do + what NOT to do)
- [ ] **Practice:** Draft 2–3 sample guest messages (reviewed by mentor)
- [ ] **Test:** Send test messages in practice environment

### Day 4–5: Live Support (Shadowing)

- [ ] **Shadow experienced agent** — 8 hours of real guest interactions
- [ ] **Observe:** How to handle different guest types + issues
- [ ] **Ask:** Questions during interactions (no customer impact)
- [ ] **Debrief:** After each major interaction

### Week 2: Independent Support (Monitored)

- [ ] **Take:** 50% of support volume (simple requests)
- [ ] **Review:** All responses reviewed by mentor before sending
- [ ] **Escalate:** Any uncertain issues to mentor immediately
- [ ] **Weekly check-in:** Performance review, feedback, coaching

### Week 3–4: Full Capacity

- [ ] **Handle:** 100% of support requests independently
- [ ] **Still:** Escalate difficult situations to mentor
- [ ] **Measure:** Response times, resolution rates, guest satisfaction
- [ ] **Monthly review:** Discuss growth areas, recognition for wins

## Training Topics & Materials

### Topic 1: Guest Communication Fundamentals

**Learning Outcome:** Ability to craft professional, empathetic guest messages

**Materials to Review:**
- All templates in 01-guest-communication-templates/
- Issue-resolution examples (06-issue-resolution-examples.md)

**Practice Exercise:**
Read guest message → Draft 3 response options → Mentor reviews → Iterate

**Example Scenario:**
> Guest: "WiFi keeps dropping. Very frustrating. Can you fix it?"
>
> **Your task:** Draft a response using principles from templates
>
> **Success criteria:**
> - Empathetic opening ("I understand, that's frustrating")
> - 2–3 actionable solutions
> - Clear next steps
> - Timeline ("should be fixed in 15 min")

---

### Topic 2: Handling Difficult Guests

**Learning Outcome:** De-escalate conflicts without authority to refund/modify bookings

**Principles:**
1. **Acknowledge their frustration** — "I understand this is frustrating"
2. **Take ownership** — "I'm here to help" (not "that's not my responsibility")
3. **Offer solutions** — Never just "sorry" with no action
4. **Escalate appropriately** — Know when to bring in team lead
5. **Document everything** — Protect yourself + team

**Case Studies to Practice:**
- Guest threatens negative review (see: Issue #6 in communication templates)
- Guest demands refund for minor issue
- Guest is rude/disrespectful to staff
- Guest feels misled about property

**Mentor Review:** Each difficult interaction reviewed + discussed

---

### Topic 3: Multi-Property Coordination

**Learning Outcome:** Manage check-ins/check-outs across 2–3 properties without overlap

**Key Concepts:**
- Calendar synchronization across platforms
- Cleaning schedule + turnover time
- Backup plans for delays
- Communication routing (which property contact, which support person)

**Tools:**
- Airbnb calendar view
- Google Sheets for master schedule
- WhatsApp groups per property

**Practice:** Mock scenario — 3 properties, 5 guests arriving/departing same day

---

### Topic 4: Issue Resolution & Escalation

**Learning Outcome:** Know which issues to solve independently vs. escalate

**Decision Tree:**

```
Guest Issue
    ↓
Is it a simple question? (appliance, WiFi, local recommendation)
    ↓ YES → Answer using templates/knowledge base
    ↓ NO
Is it a technical/maintenance issue?
    ↓ YES → Arrange contractor, communicate timeline to guest
    ↓ NO
Is it a guest complaint (noise, cleanliness, damage)?
    ↓ YES → Acknowledge + offer 2–3 solutions
            (if involves refund → escalate to manager)
    ↓ NO
Is it a safety/security emergency?
    ↓ YES → IMMEDIATE escalation + call guest + call local authorities
    ↓ NO
→ Document & follow up within 24 hours
```

**Escalation Criteria:**
- ✓ Refund/compensation decisions (manager approval required)
- ✓ Safety/security incidents
- ✓ Legal concerns (damage liability, etc.)
- ✓ Hostile/abusive guests
- ✓ Issues affecting multiple guests/properties

---

### Topic 5: Metrics & Performance Tracking

**Learning Outcome:** Understand your KPIs + how performance is measured

**Key Metrics to Monitor:**

| Metric | Target | How to Improve |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Response time | <1 hour | Set phone alerts, check every 30 min |
| Resolution rate | 80%+ | Ask mentor for help before escalating |
| Guest satisfaction | 4.8★+ | Send follow-up messages after issues |
| Accuracy | 99%+ | Double-check facts before responding |

**Monthly Review Format:**
- Response time average
- # of issues handled
- # of escalations (and why)
- Guest feedback on your support
- 1–2 areas for improvement
- Wins/recognition

## Scenario-Based Training

### Scenario 1: Guest Arrives Early (30 Min)

**Situation:** Guest texts "I'm here 30 minutes early! Can I check in now?"

**Your thought process:**
1. Check calendar — is property ready?
2. Check cleaning schedule — did cleaner finish?
3. If YES, ready → Offer early check-in + send arrival guide
4. If NO, not ready → Politely explain + offer luggage storage + welcome drink

**Training outcome:** Understand property readiness protocols + communication options

---

### Scenario 2: Broken Appliance Mid-Stay

**Situation:** Guest: "The microwave won't turn on. I need to heat up food for my kids."

**Your thought process:**
1. Express empathy ("I understand, that's frustrating with kids")
2. Troubleshoot (power outlet, button press, timer mode?)
3. If not fixable → Offer solutions:
   - Alternative: Use oven (send quick instructions)
   - Offer: Nearby restaurant delivery
   - Escalate: Call appliance repair (timeline: 2 hours)
4. Follow-up: Check if issue resolved

**Training outcome:** Prioritize guest comfort + provide alternatives when you can't fix immediately

---

### Scenario 3: Negative Review Posted (During Stay)

**Situation:** Guest left 3-star review saying "Host is not responsive"

**Your thought process:**
1. DON'T PANIC — You haven't even had chance to help yet
2. Check timeline — how long has guest been there? (Day 1? Day 3?)
3. Respond publicly (not privately) — "We're sorry you felt this way. We're here to help — please message us directly"
4. Reach out privately — apologize + ask specifically what was missing
5. Make it right — offer a solution (refund, upgrade room, credit)
6. Follow-up — ensure guest is satisfied + ask them to update review

**Training outcome:** Manage reviews professionally + convert critics into advocates

## Mentor/Coach Guidelines

### For Experienced Team Members Training New Staff

**Your role:**
- Model professional communication
- Provide constructive feedback (not just criticism)
- Celebrate wins + progress
- Escalate if mentee struggles beyond reasonable timeline

**Weekly Check-In Structure (30 min):**
1. **Wins** (5 min) — Celebrate what went well
2. **Challenges** (10 min) — Discuss difficult interactions, coaching needed
3. **Skills focus** (10 min) — Pick 1–2 areas to improve next week
4. **Resources** (5 min) — Do they need additional training?

**Sample Feedback:**
> "Great job de-escalating that guest! I loved how you offered 3 solutions. Next time, try following up 24 hours later to confirm they're satisfied — that personal touch is what drives our 5-star ratings. Let's practice that together."

## First-Month Success Checklist

By end of Month 1, new team member should be able to:

- [ ] Respond to guest inquiries within 1 hour
- [ ] Draft professional guest messages without major revisions
- [ ] Handle 80%+ of common issues independently
- [ ] Escalate appropriately (knowing when they need help)
- [ ] Document interactions clearly
- [ ] Understand all relevant SOPs
- [ ] Achieve 4.8★+ guest satisfaction feedback
- [ ] Meet or exceed response time targets

**If not meeting these targets:**
- → Additional training needed
- → Role mismatch (discuss different fit)
- → Personal circumstances affecting performance (support offered)
