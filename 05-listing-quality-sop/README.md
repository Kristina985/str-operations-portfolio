# Listing Quality Standard Operating Procedure

Comprehensive quality control framework for maintaining tier-1 listing standards, managing guest expectations, and consistently achieving top-decile review ratings.

## Overview

Listing quality has two components: the **property itself** (what guests find physically) and the **listing presentation** (what guests expect before they arrive). Gaps between expectation and reality generate negative reviews — even when the property is genuinely excellent.

This folder contains procedures to eliminate those gaps.

## Contents

### Property Quality Standards

**Cleanliness baseline**
- Pre-arrival inspection checklist (42 items: bedding, surfaces, appliances, bathrooms, floors, linens)
- Deep-clean schedule (monthly frequency + seasonal refresh)
- Guest-visible quality indicators (beds made, kitchen organized, bathroom spotless)
- Photo documentation system (dated photos of clean property before each booking)

**Amenity maintenance**
- Full kitchen equipment audit (every item that should be there, working condition)
- Appliance manual organization (heating, cooling, washing machine, stove — accessible to guests)
- WiFi reliability verification (test before each guest, backup hotspot procedure)
- Comfort items (fresh towels, quality toiletries, temperature control, quiet sleeping environment)

**Property-specific quality (Architecture & Layout)**
- Address physical constraints honestly in listing and guide
- Communicate stairs, low ceilings, compact bathrooms, noise from street
- Provide specific measurements or photos of potential concern areas
- Help guests self-select (avoid misaligned bookings from the start)

### Listing Description Optimization

**Presentation hierarchy**
1. **Core differentiators first** — Location advantage, host responsiveness, welcome pack
2. **Honest space description** — True dimensions, actual guest capacity, layout reality
3. **Amenity specificity** — Not just "kitchen equipped" but "full stove, full fridge, good counter space"
4. **Clear limitations** — Small bathroom, no outdoor space, stairs — stated positively but clearly

**Photo quality standards**
- **14–18 photos minimum** (covers all rooms, multiple angles, clear natural light)
- **Honest lighting** — Photos taken during actual natural light, not HDR or unrealistic filters
- **Detail photos** — Kitchen setup, bathroom configuration, bedroom sizes, unique character elements
- **Absence photos** — If there's no balcony, the photos shouldn't imply outdoor space; if bathroom is small, show it fully

**Description tone**
- Authoritative but warm
- Specific measurements over vague language ("compact 2m × 1.8m bathroom" not "cosy bathroom")
- Character over marketing ("authentic 18th-century stone house with original stairs" over "charming Old Town gem")
- Guest pain-points reframed as features ("winding stairs preserve the historic character; flexibility on arrival time helps guests explore")

### Review Management

**Response protocol**
- Respond to every review within 48 hours
- Address negative reviews with calm, helpful language (never defensive)
- Ask specific follow-up questions for 4-star reviews (what would have made it 5-star?)
- Thank positive reviewers and mention specific compliments they gave

**Issue prevention**
- Most negative reviews come from misaligned expectations, not property failures
- Guests who arrive surprised by bathroom size / stairs / no balcony are more critical
- Guests who arrive prepared for these features are satisfied despite them
- The November 2024 3-star review (only significant negative in 10 years) cited undisclosed limitations

**Review analysis discipline**
- Track themes across all reviews (5 dominant themes should emerge for any mature listing)
- Identify the 1–2 most-mentioned negatives (these are your listing description targets)
- Celebrate the most-mentioned positives (these are your unique value)

### Listing Presentation Checklist

**Before launch/refresh:**
- [ ] Description includes honest limitations (bathroom size, stairs, no outdoor space)
- [ ] 14–18 photos, all taken in natural light, including detail shots
- [ ] Measurements or specific descriptions of potentially concerning spaces
- [ ] Amenity list is specific (not just "kitchen equipped" but "gas stove, full fridge, dishwasher")
- [ ] Photos don't imply features you don't have (outdoor space, if absent, shouldn't be pictured)
- [ ] House rules are clear and reasonable (checkout time, noise quiet hours, guest capacity)
- [ ] Cancellation policy matches your operational needs

**Monthly maintenance:**
- [ ] Latest reviews are within 30 days (shows active property)
- [ ] All negative reviews have host responses (don't leave criticism unanswered)
- [ ] Photo quality is still accurate (update if property has changed)
- [ ] Rate is competitive vs. tier-1 comparables in your market

**Quarterly audit:**
- [ ] Review themes — what are guests consistently praising/criticizing?
- [ ] Listing description — does it accurately set expectations?
- [ ] Photo accuracy — would a guest arriving at the photos feel matched?
- [ ] Amenity actual-vs-claimed — is every listed item present and working?

## The Expectation-Reality Framework

```
POSITIVE OUTCOME:
High expectation (great photos + honest description) + Property matches expectation = Happy guest + 5-star review

NEGATIVE OUTCOME:
Low expectation set (vague listing, poor photos) + Property exceeds expectation = Surprised guest, still positive

DISASTER OUTCOME (AVOID):
High expectation (excellent photos, undersells limitations) + Property doesn't match = Disappointed guest + 3–4 star review

YOUR POSITION:
Honest high expectation (premium listing with clear limitations acknowledged) + Property matches perfectly = Premium guest experience + top-tier review
```

The key: **Be so honest about limitations that guests self-select, then exceed expectations on everything else.**
