# Listing Quality Standards – STR Operations

## 1. Accuracy of Information
- All descriptions must match the real condition of the property.
- No exaggerated claims or misleading statements.
- Amenities list must be updated before each season.
- House rules must be clear, simple, and visible.

---

## 2. Photo Standards
- Bright, natural light whenever possible.
- No clutter, no personal items.
- Horizontal photos only.
- Key areas must be included:
  - Bedroom(s)
  - Bathroom
  - Kitchen
  - Living area
  - Entrance
  - Balcony/terrace (if applicable)
- Include 1–2 detail shots for warmth (e.g., coffee setup, welcome basket).

---

## 3. Cleanliness & Preparation
- Property must be fully cleaned before every check‑in.
- Bed linens and towels must be fresh and neatly arranged.
- Trash removed, surfaces disinfected.
- Check that all lights, appliances, and WiFi work.

---

## 4. Expectation Management
- Highlight any limitations clearly:
  - Stairs
  - Noise
  - Small bathrooms
  - Older building features
- Provide honest context to avoid negative reviews.

---

## 5. Seasonal Updates
- Update photos at least once per year.
- Adjust amenities list before high season.
- Refresh description if anything changes in the property.

---

## 6. Review Monitoring
- Track guest comments for recurring issues.
- Update listing if multiple guests mention the same point.
- Use positive reviews to reinforce strong points in the description.

---

## Conclusion
Consistent listing quality ensures accurate expectations, fewer issues, and higher guest satisfaction. This SOP keeps the property aligned with professional STR standards.
