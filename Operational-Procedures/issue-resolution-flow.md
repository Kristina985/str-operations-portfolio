# Issue Resolution Flow – STR Operations

## 1. Issue Received
- Acknowledge within 5 minutes.
- Thank the guest for reporting.
- Apologize for the inconvenience.
- Ask for any missing details (photo, time, location in the apartment).

**Goal:** Guest feels heard and supported immediately.

---

## 2. Diagnose the Issue
- Identify if the issue is:
  - Access problem
  - Cleaning issue
  - Maintenance issue
  - Noise complaint
  - Amenity not working
  - Miscommunication / expectation mismatch
- Check PMS notes and previous incidents.

**Goal:** Understand the root cause quickly.

---

## 3. Provide an Immediate Workaround
Examples:
- Access issue → resend instructions, alternative entrance, backup keybox.
- Cleaning issue → send cleaner ASAP, offer temporary solution.
- Maintenance issue → guide guest through simple reset steps.
- Noise → provide guidance or contact building management.

**Goal:** Reduce guest stress and restore functionality fast.

---

## 4. Coordinate With the Appropriate Team
- Cleaning team
- Maintenance technician
- Property owner
- Building management
- PMS support (if system-related)

**Goal:** Trigger the correct operational response.

---

## 5. Follow Up With the Guest
- Confirm the issue is resolved.
- Ask if everything works now.
- Offer a small gesture if needed (late checkout, small discount, local tip).

**Goal:** Close the loop and rebuild trust.

---

## 6. Log the Incident
- Add notes in PMS.
- Tag the issue type.
- Record time to resolution.
- Note if it’s a recurring pattern.

**Goal:** Improve future operations and prevent repeat issues.

---

## Conclusion
A structured issue resolution flow ensures fast response, clear communication, and consistent guest satisfaction — even when problems occur.
