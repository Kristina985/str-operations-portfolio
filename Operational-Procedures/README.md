# Operational Procedures

Administrative and operational workflows supporting the guest-facing operations documented in other folders. These procedures ensure consistency, compliance, and accurate record-keeping.

## Overview

Excellent guest experiences depend on excellent administrative foundation. Guests don't see these procedures directly, but they impact everything from payment accuracy to issue resolution speed to legal compliance.

This folder documents:
- Calendar and booking management
- Payment and revenue tracking
- Incident documentation
- Regulatory compliance
- Data management and guest records

## Contents

### Calendar & Booking Management

**Airbnb calendar setup**
- Availability windows by season (when to open/close property for maintenance)
- Blocked dates strategy (maintenance windows, host personal time, cleaners' schedule)
- Minimum stay policies by demand tier
- Buffer time between bookings (15-min to 4-hour turnover windows)
- Last-minute availability rules (when to auto-accept, when to require host approval)

**Multi-platform calendar sync**
- Calendar integration tools (Airbnb native sync, iCal URL, channel managers like Smoobu)
- Double-booking prevention protocol
- Booking confirmation backup (screenshot for records)
- Guest information capture (phone, email, special requests)

### Payment & Revenue Tracking

**Payment reconciliation**
- Monthly revenue audit (bookings booked vs. payouts received)
- Fee structure tracking (post-October 2025 Airbnb changes, platform-specific fees)
- Currency conversion documentation (if multi-currency bookings)
- Tax withholding tracking (country-specific reporting requirements)

**Income documentation**
- Annual revenue summary (for tax/visa purposes)
- Booking-by-booking breakdown (date, guest name, nightly rate, total, fees)
- Occupancy rate calculation (booked nights ÷ available nights)
- ADR calculation (total revenue ÷ booked nights)

**Tax and legal documentation**
- Self-employment income reporting (varies by country: US Schedule C, UK Self-Assessment, Croatian forms)
- Quarterly estimated taxes (if applicable)
- VAT/GST considerations (if business crosses threshold)
- Guest records for liability purposes

### Incident & Issue Documentation

**Issue log template**
- Date and time of issue
- Issue description (WiFi down, guest concern, property damage, noise complaint)
- Resolution taken and timeline
- Host communication record (what was sent to guest, when)
- Outcome (issue resolved, guest compensation if applicable)

**Damage documentation**
- Photo evidence (timestamps on device, backup copy)
- Guest communication (how issue was addressed in real-time)
- Timeline (when discovered, when reported to guest)
- Resolution (repair cost, guest accountability if applicable, platform report if needed)

**Communication records**
- All messages with guests (backup outside of platform)
- Issue resolution correspondence
- Payment/cancellation communications
- Review responses

### Guest Record Management

**Privacy and data protection**
- Guest information storage (secure, backed up)
- Data retention policy (how long to keep guest contact info after stay)
- GDPR/CCPA compliance (if hosts are EU/US-based)
- Guest communication archive (messages, special requests, health notes)

**Guest profile for repeat bookings**
- Arrival preferences (early check-in, parking notes)
- Accessibility requirements (mobility assistance, ground floor preference)
- Dietary information (if welcome pack is customised)
- Communication preferences (phone vs. email, language)

### Compliance & Regulatory

**Airbnb Superhost requirements tracking**
- Response rate requirement (≥90% messages within 24 hours)
- Cancellation rate (≤1% for month-long policies)
- Rating requirement (4.8+ average)
- Review frequency (at least 50% of booked guests review you)
- Monthly Superhost status check

**Platform-specific policies**
- House rules enforcement (quiet hours, guest capacity, parking restrictions)
- Cancellation policy compliance (stated policy vs. actual practice)
- Guest communication standards (response time, professionalism)
- Photo accuracy and listing description truthfulness

**Local regulatory compliance** (varies by country/region)
- Tourist registration requirements (Croatia: e-Hosts system)
- Tax registration and reporting
- Insurance requirements (landlord, liability, property coverage)
- Occupancy limits and safety standards
- Local rental law restrictions (length of stay minimums, seasonal restrictions)

### Monthly Operations Checklist

- [ ] Revenue reconciliation (bookings vs. payouts match)
- [ ] Superhost metrics check (response rate, cancellations, reviews, rating)
- [ ] Calendar audit (blocked dates set, minimums configured, turnover time reasonable)
- [ ] Outstanding issues resolved (no unresponded guest messages older than 24 hours)
- [ ] Guest records updated (repeat guest notes, preferences, special requests)
- [ ] Tax documentation organised (monthly backup of revenue records)
- [ ] Competitor rate tracking (quarterly, but tracked monthly)

### Quarterly Operations Review

- [ ] Year-to-date revenue vs. projection
- [ ] Occupancy rate vs. target (top-10% tier = 80%+)
- [ ] Average review rating trend
- [ ] Incident frequency (are problems increasing/decreasing?)
- [ ] Platform policy changes (Airbnb fee structure, local regulations)
- [ ] Pricing adjustment needed (based on market movement)

## Important: Legal Compliance Varies by Location

These procedures are general guidelines. Your specific obligations depend on:
- **Country of residence** (where you are taxed)
- **Country of property location** (where local laws apply)
- **Booking platform requirements** (Airbnb terms, Booking.com standards)

**Strongly recommended:** Consult with a tax professional and local legal advisor familiar with STR regulations in your jurisdiction. Non-compliance can result in:
- Tax penalties and back taxes
- Fines from local authorities
- Property seizure (in some jurisdictions)
- Platform suspension
