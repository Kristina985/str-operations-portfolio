# Check-In / Check-Out Standard Operating Procedures

Complete workflow documentation for arrival and departure operations. These procedures ensure consistency, eliminate guest confusion, and maintain property standards.

## Overview

Check-in and check-out are critical moments in the guest experience — they set the emotional tone for the stay and determine final impressions. This folder contains the procedures that have maintained a 9.6+ check-in satisfaction score across 100+ reviews.

## Contents

### Pre-Check-In (48–72 hours before arrival)

- **Guest contact confirmation** — Verify phone number, arrival time, ground transport method
- **Property preparation checklist** — Cleaning, amenities restocking, temperature control
- **Access preparation** — Keybox PIN generation, backup key placement, emergency contact lamination
- **Welcome pack assembly** — Local food items, spirits, welcome note with host photo

### Check-In Process

- **30 minutes before arrival** — Brief SMS confirming PIN and building entrance photo
- **Keybox protocol** — PIN delivery method, backup access options, lost PIN procedures
- **Property orientation** — Where keys go, WiFi credentials, thermostat settings, appliance quick-start
- **Walkthrough script** — Multi-floor navigation, emergency exits, water heater location
- **Welcome pack presentation** — Placement, dietary note, local recommendations included
- **Flexibility option** — Early check-in accommodation policy and booking confirmation

### During-Stay Support

- **Emergency contact system** — 24-hour availability, escalation protocol
- **Issue response SLA** — WiFi/appliance issues resolved within 2 hours, same-day
- **Mid-stay check-in** — Day 2–3, "Is everything perfect?" proactive outreach

### Check-Out Process

- **Departure reminder** — 24 hours ahead with checkout time and process
- **Flexible checkout offer** — Based on next booking, usually 12pm or later
- **Key return protocol** — Leave on table, no staff required, simple and dignified
- **Final inspection checklist** — Appliances off, doors/windows secured, lights off
- **Damage/loss protocol** — What to flag, how to handle, communication timeline

### Post-Check-Out

- **Turnover protocol** — Time allocation for cleaning before next guest, contingency for late departure
- **Incident documentation** — Damage reports, missing items, guest communication records
- **Review readiness** — Timing for review requests, response to any concerns raised during stay

## Performance Data

These procedures have achieved:
- **96%+ check-in satisfaction** across all guest reviews
- **Zero check-out disputes** in 10+ years of hosting
- **2-hour emergency response time** (100% compliance)
- **0 security incidents** — despite keybox access, never a misused PIN or theft

## Key Differentiators

✓ **Video walkthroughs** — Appliance instruction videos shared pre-arrival eliminate support emails  
✓ **Illustrated guide** — Photo-marked arrival route ensures guests navigate confidently  
✓ **Flexible timing** — Early check-in and late checkout based on actual schedule, not rigid policy  
✓ **Proactive support** — Mid-stay check-in catches issues before they become review problems  

## Adapting for Your Property

1. **Measure your current baseline** — Check-in satisfaction, issue response time, checkout friction
2. **Identify your property's specific needs** — Stairs, appliances, parking, unique access challenges
3. **Create your walkthrough video** — Even 90 seconds on your smartphone is powerful
4. **Test one procedure at a time** — Implement mid-stay check-in first, measure impact, add others
5. **Track satisfaction before/after** — Review ratings, guest comments, support message volume
